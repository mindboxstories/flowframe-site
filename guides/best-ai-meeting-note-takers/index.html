Best AI Meeting Note-Takers in 2026: Fathom vs Granola vs Otter vs Fireflies

  • Updated 2026-11-08
  • Prices checked Nov 2026

Quick answer: If you want a free note-taker you can rely on every day, start with Fathom. If you hate having a bot join your calls, use Granola. If you mostly need searchable transcripts of long calls, Otter Pro is the best value on an annual plan. Fireflies suits teams that want notes pushed into a CRM or Slack automatically.

How we built this guide: we compared each tool's current plans, free-tier limits and how it joins your meetings, using the vendors' published pricing and recent independent reviews (prices checked November 2026). We are adding hands-on test notes to each pick as we record our own calls, and we date every update below.

1. Fathom: the best free AI note-taker

Fathom's free plan is the most generous of the four: unlimited recording, transcription in more than 25 languages and unlimited storage. The catch is that the richer AI summaries and action items are limited to five calls a month, after which you get a basic summary.

Choose it if you have a handful of important calls a week and want a free tool that never cuts you off mid-meeting.

Skip it if you need advanced summaries on every call; that is where Premium (about $16/month on an annual plan) comes in.

2. Granola: best if you don't want a bot in the room

Granola doesn't send a bot into your meeting. It captures audio from your own computer and turns your rough notes into a clean write-up. Clients and colleagues never see a "notetaker has joined" message, which makes it the easiest choice for sensitive or external calls.

Choose it if you take your own notes and want AI to tidy and complete them.

Skip it if you want a shared recording your whole team can replay; bot-based tools do that better. Business is $14/month and is billed monthly only.

3. Otter: best value for long calls

Otter is the veteran of the group and is built around the transcript itself: searchable, editable and shareable. The free plan caps you at 300 minutes a month and 30 minutes per conversation, which is too tight for most real meetings. Pro raises that to 1,200 minutes and 90 minutes per conversation, and on annual billing it is the cheapest paid plan here at $8.33/month.

Choose it if you record lectures, interviews or long meetings and want to search what was said.

Skip it if you only need short summaries; Fathom's free plan covers that.

4. Fireflies: best for teams that live in their CRM

Fireflies stands out for where it sends your notes: CRMs, Slack, project tools and more. The free plan is mainly a trial because storage and AI credits are limited. Pro starts at $10/month on annual billing, and Business at $19/month adds the team features most companies buy it for.

Choose it if you want meeting notes to land automatically in your sales or project tools.

Skip it if you are a solo user; the value is in the integrations.

How to choose in 30 seconds

Spending nothing? Fathom free.

Don't want a bot visible? Granola.

Long recordings and transcripts? Otter Pro, annual.

Team workflow and CRM? Fireflies Business.

A note on privacy: tell people when a meeting is being recorded. Some countries and many employers require consent, whichever tool you use.
