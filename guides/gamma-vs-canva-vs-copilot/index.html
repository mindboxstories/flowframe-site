Gamma vs Canva vs Copilot: Which AI Presentation Maker Should You Use in 2026?

  • Updated 2026-11-08

Quick answer: For a fast, good-looking first draft from a single prompt, use Gamma. If your slides must match a brand or double as social posts, use Canva. If you already pay for Microsoft 365 and your company lives in PowerPoint, use Copilot in PowerPoint, which turns a Word document into a deck in your own template.

1. Gamma: best for a fast first draft

Type a topic or paste your notes and Gamma builds a complete, well-designed deck in about a minute, with layouts and images already in place. The free plan gives you 400 AI credits when you sign up and up to 10 slides per prompt, which is enough to try it properly. Plus removes the Gamma branding and allows much longer decks.

Choose it if you want a polished draft quickly and are happy to present from a link or export to PowerPoint.

Skip it if you need pixel-perfect control over every layout; Gamma's card-based design is fast but less flexible, and its style can feel informal for very formal audiences.

2. Canva: best when the deck must look on-brand

Canva's strength is design: a huge template library, brand kits with your fonts and colours, and easy reuse of slides as Instagram or LinkedIn posts. Its AI tools (Magic Design and friends) can generate a starting deck, and the free plan includes a small monthly AI allowance. Pro raises that allowance and unlocks brand kits and premium templates.

Choose it if you care about looks, you work in a team, or you want one design to serve slides and social media.

Skip it if you want the AI to do most of the thinking. Canva's AI gets you started, but you will do more of the arranging yourself than with Gamma.

3. Copilot in PowerPoint: best if you already live in Microsoft 365

Copilot works inside PowerPoint itself. Its standout trick is turning an existing Word document into a deck, and on work accounts it can use your organisation's own template. Microsoft 365 Personal and Family now include a monthly allowance of AI credits for the account owner, so many people already have it.

Choose it if you already pay for Microsoft 365, or your workplace requires PowerPoint files and templates.

Skip it if you don't use Microsoft 365. There is no free version, and business Copilot is a costly add-on on top of a Microsoft 365 plan.

How to choose in 30 seconds

Need a deck in 5 minutes? Gamma.

Need it on-brand, or reused on social media? Canva.

Already in Microsoft 365, or company template required? Copilot in PowerPoint.

Spending nothing? Start with Gamma's free credits, then finish in Canva free.
