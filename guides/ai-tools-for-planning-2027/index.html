7 AI Tools for Planning 2027 (and What Each One Costs)

FlowFrame teamUpdated 12 Nov 2026Prices checked Nov 2026

Disclosure: FlowFrame may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. This never changes our picks. Read more.

Quick answer: Use Reclaim.ai to protect focus time on your calendar and MeetGeek so you stop taking meeting notes. Run the plan itself in ClickUp if you work alone or with a free team, or monday.com if your team wants visual boards. Then use Gamma, Canva and ElevenLabs to turn the plan into a deck, graphics and a voiceover. All seven have a free plan, so you can build the whole stack for $0 and only pay where you hit a limit.

How we built this guide: we compared each tool's free plan, its published starting price and its main limit, using the vendors' own pricing pages (checked 12 November 2026). Plan your time: Reclaim.ai and MeetGeek

1. Reclaim.ai: a calendar that defends your focus time

Reclaim books focus blocks, habits and breaks around your meetings, then moves them when a new meeting lands, so your plan survives a busy week. It works with Google Calendar and Outlook. The free Lite plan only schedules one week ahead and connects two calendars; Starter is $10 per seat a month on annual billing ($12 monthly).

Choose it if your calendar fills up before your own work gets done.

Skip it if your workplace won't allow a tool full access to your calendar.

2. MeetGeek: meeting notes you don't have to write

MeetGeek joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls (with a bot or bot-free), records them and writes a summary with action items. The free Basic plan includes 3 hours of transcription a month and keeps transcripts for 3 months. Pro is listed at $9.99 to $15.99 per user a month depending on billing.

Choose it if you spend more than a few hours a week in calls.

Skip it if you rarely meet; the free plan will be enough.

Run the plan: monday.com or ClickUp

3. monday.com: visual boards for a team

monday.com turns goals into colourful boards, timelines and dashboards that a whole team can read at a glance. The free plan covers up to 2 seats and 3 boards. Paid plans start at $9 per seat a month on annual billing, with a 3-seat minimum.

Choose it if you plan with a team that wants to see progress visually.

Skip it if you work alone; the 3-seat minimum means paying for empty seats.

4. ClickUp: tasks, docs and goals in one workspace

ClickUp puts tasks, docs, goals, calendars and time tracking in one place. Its Free Forever plan allows unlimited tasks and members, with 60 MB of storage. Core starts at $9 per user a month on annual billing.

Choose it if you want one free tool for the whole year's plan.

Skip it if you want something simple; ClickUp has a lot of features, so set up one space first.

Share the plan: Gamma, Canva and ElevenLabs

5. Gamma: a deck from an outline

Paste your plan or an outline and Gamma drafts a presentation you can edit. The free plan gives 400 one-off credits and up to 10 slides per prompt; Plus adds 1,500 credits a month and Pro 4,000. Annual plans are cheaper than monthly.

Choose it if you present plans and hate building slides.

Skip it if you need decks that match a strict brand template.

6. Canva: graphics for every channel

Canva designs and resizes graphics for slides, social posts and printouts from one design. The free plan is generous. Pro costs $119.99 a year or $12.99 a month and covers up to 5 people.

Choose it if you share your plan as visuals or social posts.

Skip it if you only need plain documents.

7. ElevenLabs: a voiceover from a script

ElevenLabs turns a written script into a natural-sounding voiceover for videos, course lessons or team updates. The free plan gives 10,000 credits (about 10 minutes) a month but no commercial licence; Starter adds one for $6 a month.

Choose it if you make videos or audio updates and don't want to record your own voice.

Skip it if you need it for paid work on the free plan; you'll need Starter for the licence. Always say when a voice is AI-generated.

How to choose in 30 seconds

Spending nothing? Reclaim Lite, MeetGeek Basic, ClickUp Free, Gamma, Canva and ElevenLabs free plans.

Working with a team? monday.com for boards, ClickUp if you want it free.

One upgrade only? Reclaim Starter, if your calendar is the problem.

Making videos? ElevenLabs Starter for the commercial licence.
