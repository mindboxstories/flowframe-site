7 AI Tools for Planning 2027 (and What Each One Costs)

By FlowFrame · Updated 2026-11-11

Disclosure: FlowFrame may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. This never changes our picks. Read more.

The short version: start with Reclaim.ai to protect focus time and MeetGeek so you stop taking meeting notes. Use monday.com or ClickUp for the plan itself, and Gamma, Canva and ElevenLabs to turn it into decks, graphics and voiceovers. All seven have a free plan or free tier.

How we picked: we compared each tool's free plan and published starting price (November 2026). We are adding hands-on screen-recording notes to each pick and date every update below.

Calendar and meetings

1. Reclaim.ai, free plan

Auto-schedules focus time, breaks and habits around your meetings, and moves them when a new meeting lands.
The catch: it needs full access to your calendar to work, which some workplaces restrict.
See Reclaim.ai

2. MeetGeek, free plan; paid from $9.99/user/month (annual)

Joins your calls, records, and writes the summary and action items.
The catch: it joins as a bot, which some clients notice.
See MeetGeek

The plan itself

3. monday.com, free plan; paid per seat

Visual boards for team plans, timelines and dashboards.
The catch: paid plans have seat minimums, so small teams can pay for empty seats.
See monday.com

4. ClickUp, free plan; paid per seat

Tasks, docs and notes in one workspace.
The catch: there are a lot of features; set up one simple space first.
See ClickUp

Sharing the plan

5. Gamma, free plan

Turns a prompt or an outline into a presentation.
The catch: decks look similar unless you adjust the theme.
See Gamma

6. Canva, free; Pro paid

Design and resize graphics for every platform from one design.
The catch: the best templates and AI features need Pro.
See Canva

7. ElevenLabs, free tier

Turns a script into a natural-sounding voiceover.
The catch: disclose AI voices in videos and ads.
See ElevenLabs
