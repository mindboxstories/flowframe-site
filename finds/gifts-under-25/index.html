12 Gifts Under $25 People Are Actually Buying This Holiday Season

By Digittouch Finds · Updated 2026-11-08

The short version: the gifts under $25 that keep showing up on this season's best-seller and editors' lists are small tech (trackers, budget earbuds, speakers), cosy winter comforts, easy kitchen upgrades and mini versions of cult beauty products. Below are 12 we would actually give, each with the catch.

How we picked: we cross-checked this season's best-seller and editors' gift lists (November 2026) and kept items that appear repeatedly, have strong buyer reviews and work for a wide range of people. Prices are approximate and change daily, especially around Black Friday, so check the current price before you buy.

Tech gifts

1. Apple AirTag (2nd generation), about $23

Clip it to keys, a bag or a suitcase and find it from an iPhone. A near-perfect "I didn't know I needed this" gift.
The catch: it only works with iPhones. For Android users, buy a Samsung SmartTag or a Tile instead.

2. Anker Soundcore noise-cancelling earbuds, about $25

Noise cancelling, a transparency mode and around 10 hours of playtime, at a price that makes them easy to give.
The catch: budget noise cancelling softens a commute but won't match $200 earbuds.

3. Waterproof Bluetooth speaker, about $20

Waterproof, with a battery rated around 24 hours. Good for the shower, the beach or a teenager's room.
The catch: small speakers are loud enough for a room, not a party.

Quick picks by person

  • For the person who loses everything: AirTag (iPhone) or a Tile (Android).
  • For the cook: French press or bamboo board set.
