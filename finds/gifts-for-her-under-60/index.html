11 Gift Ideas for Her Under $60 (Before Black Friday 2026)

The short version: the gifts for her that keep appearing on this season's gift guides are cosy comforts, small beauty treats, a good tumbler, a plant she can't kill and a piece of jewellery. Here are 11, grouped by budget, each with one honest catch.

How we picked: we cross-checked the 2026 gift guides from major shopping editors with this week's Amazon best-sellers, and kept items that appear repeatedly and suit a wide range of people. Prices were checked on 11 November 2026 and change daily around Black Friday (27 November), so check the price before you buy. We only call something a deal when we have seen the price drop.

Under $25

1. Fuzzy faux-fur slippers, about $16

Soft, open-toe house slippers that turn up on almost every winter gift list. An easy pick when you don't know her taste well.
The catch: sizes run in pairs (for example 7-8), so if she's between sizes, go up.

2. Candle warmer lamp, about $20

A small lamp that melts a candle from the top, so there is no open flame. A good add-on to a candle gift.
The catch: you still need to buy the candle; check the jar fits under the lamp.

3. P.F. Candle Co. Woody Spruce candle, about $24

A soy candle with a winter forest scent in a simple glass jar that looks good on a shelf.
The catch: fragrance is personal; if you're unsure, pick a fresh or citrus scent instead.

A mulberry silk mask that blocks light for naps, flights and late mornings.

A large insulated tumbler with a built-in straw and a side handle. Owala's colourways sell out every holiday season.

A gift set of travel-size Summer Fridays favourites in one pouch. A good pick for someone who travels.

7. Salt & Stone body wash, Bergamot & Hinoki, about $36

A body wash with a clean, woody scent in a bottle nice enough to keep on show.
